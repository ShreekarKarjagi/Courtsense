Court Sense

New to volleyball rotations? Start in Learn mode: pick Serving or Serve Receive, step through Rotations 1–6, and watch all six players snap into their correct spot — no arranging needed, just study the shape. Once it clicks, switch to Test mode, where the court is scrambled and you drag each player into a legal formation yourself. Behind the scenes, every formation follows volleyball's overlap rule: at the instant of serve, teammates must hold a fixed left-to-right and front-to-back order — Court Sense shows you exactly what that looks like, rotation by rotation.
